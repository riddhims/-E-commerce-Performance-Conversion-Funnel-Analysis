🛒 E-commerce Performance & Conversion Funnel Analysis

📌 Overview
This project analyzes Google Analytics 4 (GA4) data to evaluate e-commerce website performance, user engagement, acquisition channels, conversion behavior, and revenue.
The dashboard covers the period September 28 – October 6, 2026.
The goal is to transform website analytics data into actionable business insights across the customer journey:
Traffic → Engagement → Product Interaction → Cart → Checkout → Purchase

🎯 Business Questions
Where does website traffic come from?
How engaged are website visitors?
Which pages receive the most traffic?
How do users move through the e-commerce funnel?
Where does user drop-off occur?
How many purchases are generated?
How much revenue is generated?
Which areas should be investigated to improve conversion?

🔎 Key Insights
Traffic Acquisition
Direct traffic was the largest acquisition source, representing 67.2% of sessions, followed by Google organic search at 23.5%.
Conversion Funnel
The analysis tracks the customer journey from product interaction through purchase:
View Item → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase
The dashboard recorded 66,962 product-view events, 14,155 add-to-cart events, and 1,142 purchases.
Revenue
The website generated approximately $165.5K in purchase revenue, with an average order value of $145.
Purchase revenue increased by 166.9% compared with the previous period.
Top Pages
The homepage generated the highest number of page views, followed by the checkout page and several product/category pages.


💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the available data, potential areas for further investigation include:
Checkout abandonment
Traffic-source conversion rates
New vs. returning user behavior
Product/category conversion performance
Mobile vs. desktop behavior
High-traffic pages with low conversion
Customer journey optimization
These are investigation opportunities rather than confirmed causes, since the dashboard does not provide enough information to establish why users drop out of the funnel.
